## **6.1 — Definition**
A **Serenity Hook** is a formal, non‑activating semantic binding between:

- the Logic Document  
- the Serenity‑Spectral Runtime  
- the spectral operator families  
- the topology envelope  
- the integration semantics  

Hooks **do not activate** Serenity.  
They only define **where** Serenity *could* attach.

## **6.4 — Hook Validity Rules**
A Serenity Hook is **valid** when:

- integration semantics are valid (Section 5)  
- error semantics are stable (Section 4)  
- admissibility is satisfied (Section 3.3)  
- state semantics are coherent (Section 3.1)  
- topology is continuous (Section 3.6)  
- chart transitions are reversible (Section 3.5)  
- spectral fibers are compatible (Section 3.4)  
- constraints are satisfied (Section 3.2)  

A hook is **invalid** when any rule fails.

Invalid hooks must not be used for runtime binding.
