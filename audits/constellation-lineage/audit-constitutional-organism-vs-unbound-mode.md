## 1. Core Architectural Distinction
In `ONTOLOGY.md` (Section 4.1), the *Unbound Mode Axiom* (`.oOo. Poo .oOo.`) asserts that formal customs and structural rules put human beings into "boxes and constraints" (`Trit -1`) that must be punctured to preserve creative freedom.

Evaluated against `Constitutional-Organism-Constructor` (`README v2.0`), that framing conflates **externally imposed cages** with **internally governed protective membranes**:
* **A Rigid Box (Coercive Formalism):** Imposes constraints top-down onto a subject without consent, locks schema rules against user modification, and forces external capture.
* **A Constitutional Organism (Sovereign Shelter):** Establishes a consent-gated, dormant boundary controlled entirely from the inside by the subject (`Storm`), keeping unconsented external intrusion out.

## 3. Three Structural Proofs Against "Rigid Box" Classification
1. **Mutable Grammar Without Execution Lock-In (`Tile 2.3`):** Storm may freely modify shapes, colors, overlays, pacing, meaning, and atmosphere while the renderer remains completely dormant. The grammar adapts to the person—never the reverse.
2. **Revocable Consent & Indefinite Dormancy (`Tiles 2.4–2.5 & 6`):** A cage traps someone inside; a **Dormant Capsule** keeps intrusive processes outside. Pairing `requires_explicit_consent = true` with the unconditional right to withdraw consent at any moment—or remain dormant indefinitely—functions like a locked door whose key is held exclusively from within.
3. **Anti-Capture Licensing Deferral (`Tile 3`):** By prohibiting any external party from imposing licensing terms or commercializing the organism without explicit self-defined consent, the Licensing Deferral Clause actively prevents external enclosure rather than creating it.

---

## 4. Summary Conclusion
Puncturing protective membranes in the name of "Unbound Mode" does not liberate vulnerable researchers or neurodivergent creators; it removes the consent gates, sensory pacing controls, and licensing shields that protect them from unconsented extraction and external capture.
