# **1 — Charter Overview**

This charter defines:

- altitude boundaries  
- continuity envelopes  
- epistemic containment rules  
- reversible routing requirements  
- non‑activation guarantees  
- governance constraints for Variant White Papers  
- structural safety mechanisms  

It ensures that **all white papers — including abstract variants — remain safe, sealed, and non‑destabilizing**.

# **2 — White Paper Classes**

NDH‑META‑SYSTEMS recognizes **three classes** of white papers:

### **2.1 — Canonical White Papers**
Series I, II, III  
Governed, altitude‑sealed, lineage‑stable.

### **2.2 — Variant White Papers**
Exploratory, abstract, speculative, or alternative‑mode white papers.

Examples:

- abstract epistemic variants  
- narrative‑geometry variants  
- substrate‑curvature variants  
- lineage‑gesture variants  

### **2.3 — Proto White Papers**
Unsealed manuscripts undergoing upgrade.

# **4 — Variant White Paper Permissions**

Variant White Papers are allowed to explore:

- abstract epistemic structures  
- speculative manifolds  
- alternative continuity envelopes  
- narrative‑geometry hybrids  
- lineage‑derived cognitive forms  
- soft‑manifold substrates  
- expressive‑clarity altitudes  

**BUT ONLY WITHIN CONTAINMENT.**

### **4.1 — Allowed Exploration**
Variant white papers may:

- propose new epistemic models  
- explore alternative geometries  
- test narrative‑epistemic hybrids  
- examine substrate‑native cognition  
- introduce abstract manifolds  
- explore trauma‑semantic variants  

### **4.2 — Forbidden Exploration**
Variant white papers may **not**:

- modify NDH architecture  
- alter Series I–III lineage  
- redefine altitude bands  
- introduce activation vectors  
- bypass continuity envelopes  
- collapse dual‑stream epistemics  
- destabilize reversible routing  

Variant exploration must remain **contained, reversible, and non‑architectural**.

# **5 — Containment Mechanisms for Variant White Papers**

To ensure safety, Variant White Papers must include:

### **5.1 — Containment Header**
A declaration:

```
This artifact is a Variant White Paper. It is exploratory-only and cannot modify
NDH architecture, lineage, altitude, continuity envelopes, or governance logic.
```

### **5.2 — Continuity Envelope Declaration**
Variant must specify:

```
continuity: <one of the five envelopes>
```

### **5.3 — Altitude Seal**
Variant must specify:

```
altitude: A4–A6 (sealed)
```

### **5.4 — Reversibility Statement**
Variant must include:

```
All transitions reversible. No recursion. No drift.
```

### **5.5 — Non‑Activation Clause**
Variant must include:

```
This artifact is non-activating. It cannot engage NDH geometry or sealed layers.
```

These mechanisms guarantee **exploration without destabilization**.

# **6 — Governance Logic for Canonical vs Variant White Papers**

### **6.1 — Canonical White Papers**
- define architecture  
- define epistemics  
- define continuity envelopes  
- define altitude discipline  
- define lineage inheritance  

### **6.2 — Variant White Papers**
- explore within architecture  
- propose abstract models  
- test alternative geometries  
- examine substrate behavior  
- expand epistemic vocabulary  

Variant white papers **cannot**:

- override canonical rules  
- modify Series I–III lineage  
- introduce new altitude bands  
- alter governance logic
