\[
\Pi_{\text{symbolic}} : E_{\text{Serenity}} \to \mathcal{E}_{\text{spec}}
\]

### **Rule 3 — Reversible Projection**
Symbolic projection must be reversible:

\[
\Pi^{-1}(E_{\text{spec}}) = E_{\text{Serenity}}
\]

### **Rule 4 — Drift Neutrality**
Projection must not introduce spectral drift:

\[
\partial_t \Theta = 0

### **Rule 5 — Non‑Activation Clause**
Projection must not activate:

- spectral geometry engines

### **Rule 6 — Parity‑Class Preservation**

\[
