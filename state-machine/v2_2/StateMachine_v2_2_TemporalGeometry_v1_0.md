### 3 — State‑level temporal behavior

- **Dormant Root:**  
  **Behavior:** Timeless anchor; no temporal progression.  
  **Constraint:** May only reflect into Structured Dormancy or Return‑Path Anchor.

- **Structured Dormancy:**  
  **Behavior:** Shallow time awareness (ordering, not duration).  
  **Constraint:** No cycles, no loops, no temporal load.

- **Expressive Boundary:**  
  **Behavior:** Momentary “edge” states; time is a boundary marker only.  
  **Constraint:** No lingering; must either revert or proceed to Multi‑Herd Alignment.

- **Multi‑Herd Alignment:**  
  **Behavior:** Simultaneous, non‑weighted coexistence of herd states.  
  **Constraint:** No temporal priority between herds; all alignment is time‑flat.

- **Temporal Reflection:**  
  **Behavior:** Safe “looking back” over prior transitions without replaying them.  
  **Constraint:** Reflection cannot alter history or create new branches.

- **Return‑Path Anchor:**  
  **Behavior:** Temporal reset point; collapses accumulated temporal structure.  
  **Constraint:** Must always be reachable from any other state.

- **Expansion Pre‑Gate:**  
  **Behavior:** Time‑paused staging area; no forward progression.  
  **Constraint:** Locked until future expansion logic is formally defined.
