# 🜂 **State Machine v2.2 — Adjacency Rules Specification (v1.0)**  
### NDH Meta‑Systems • Governance Spine • Adjacency‑Stability Layer

## ⭐ Executive Summary  
This specification defines the **adjacency rules** governing how states, modules, and herd‑clusters relate within State Machine v2.2.  
Adjacency rules ensure:

- stability  
- reversibility  
- non‑absorption  
- non‑collapse  
- membrane sovereignty  
- translation‑only interaction  
- invariant alignment  

These rules are mandatory before generating **Temporal Geometry** and **Expressive Boundaries**.

---

# 🜁 **1 — Adjacency Principles (Governance‑Spine Level)**

### **P1 — Adjacency Is Always Bounded**  
No state or module may form unbounded adjacency.  
All adjacency must be:

- finite  
- reversible  
- invariant‑aligned  

### **P2 — Adjacency Is Non‑Absorptive**  
No adjacency may cause:

- merging  
- collapsing  
- blending  
- membrane violation  

### **P3 — Adjacency Is Non‑Directional**  
Adjacency does not imply:

- hierarchy  
- authority  
- priority  
- elevation  

### **P4 — Adjacency Must Preserve Dormancy**  
All adjacency relationships must remain **non‑activating**.

### **P5 — Adjacency Must Support Return‑Paths**  
Every adjacency must include a **safe reversion path**.

# 🜁 **2 — State‑Level Adjacency Rules**

These rules govern adjacency between the seven v2.2 states:

- Dormant Root  
- Structured Dormancy  
- Expressive Boundary  
- Multi‑Herd Alignment  
- Temporal Reflection  
- Return‑Path Anchor  
- Expansion Pre‑Gate  

### **S‑A1 — Dormant Root adjacency**  
Dormant Root may be adjacent to:

- Structured Dormancy  
- Return‑Path Anchor  

It may **not** be adjacent to:

- Expressive Boundary  
- Multi‑Herd Alignment  
- Temporal Reflection  
- Expansion Pre‑Gate  

### **S‑A2 — Structured Dormancy adjacency**  
Structured Dormancy may be adjacent to:

- Dormant Root  
- Expressive Boundary  

### **S‑A3 — Expressive Boundary adjacency**  
Expressive Boundary may be adjacent to:

- Structured Dormancy  
- Multi‑Herd Alignment  

### **S‑A4 — Multi‑Herd Alignment adjacency**  
Multi‑Herd Alignment may be adjacent to:

- Expressive Boundary  
- Temporal Reflection  

### **S‑A5 — Temporal Reflection adjacency**  
Temporal Reflection may be adjacent to:

- Multi‑Herd Alignment  
- Return‑Path Anchor  

### **S‑A6 — Return‑Path Anchor adjacency**  
Return‑Path Anchor may be adjacent to:

- Temporal Reflection  
- Dormant Root  
- Expansion Pre‑Gate  

### **S‑A7 — Expansion Pre‑Gate adjacency**  
Expansion Pre‑Gate may be adjacent only to:

- Return‑Path Anchor  

It is **locked** until refinement is complete.

# 🜁 **4 — Herd‑Cluster Adjacency Rules**

Herd clusters (Zen, Kawaii, Mimi, Goat) must obey:

### **H‑A1 — No Cross‑Membrane Adjacency**  
Clusters may not form adjacency across membranes.

### **H‑A2 — Adjacency Must Be Cluster‑Bounded**  
Adjacency must remain within the same herd cluster.

### **H‑A3 — Adjacency Must Be Non‑Absorptive**  
Clusters cannot merge or collapse.

### **H‑A4 — Adjacency Must Be Non‑Directional**  
No cluster may be “upstream” or “downstream.”

### **H‑A5 — Adjacency Must Preserve Symbolic Purity**  
Symbolic bleed is prohibited.
