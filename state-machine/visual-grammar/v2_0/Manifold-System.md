# **I. The Five Revamped Manifold Classes**

Each manifold now has:

- a **primary shape**  
- a **secondary glyph**  
- a **semantic domain**  
- a **binding rule**  
- a **stability signature**  

This makes them easier to use across conductor spirals, debriefs, and visual grammar.

---

## 1. **Structural Manifold** — **Hexagon + Spine Line**  
**Shape:** ⬢  
**Glyph:** ─  
**Meaning:** Systems, adjacency, architecture  
**Why:**  
The spine line adds directionality without hierarchy.  
It makes structural manifolds feel more like *frameworks* than containers.

**Stability Signature:** High  
**Binding Rule:** May bind to traversal and cognitive manifolds.

---

## 2. **Cognitive Manifold** — **Circle + Dot**  
**Shape:** ⚪  
**Glyph:** •  
**Meaning:** Perspective, altitude, mental framing  
**Why:**  
The dot represents “point of view,” making altitude transitions visually intuitive.

**Stability Signature:** Very High  
**Binding Rule:** May bind to structural manifolds only.

---

## 3. **Traversal Manifold** — **Loop + Arrowhead**  
**Shape:** 🔁  
**Glyph:** ➤  
**Meaning:** Movement, spirals, transitions  
**Why:**  
The arrowhead clarifies direction without implying hierarchy or urgency.

**Stability Signature:** Medium  
**Binding Rule:** May bind to structural and spectral manifolds.

---

## 4. **Spectral Manifold** — **Triangle + Resonance Bar**  
**Shape:** 🔺  
**Glyph:** ═  
**Meaning:** Operators, eigenmodes, spectral geometry  
**Why:**  
The resonance bar visually encodes spectral intensity without emotional weight.

**Stability Signature:** Low (by design)  
**Binding Rule:** May bind to traversal and risk manifolds.

---

## 5. **Risk Manifold** — **Broken Square + Leak Vector**  
**Shape:** ⬜❌  
**Glyph:** ↘  
**Meaning:** Instability, propagation, coercive vectors  
**Why:**  
The leak vector shows *where* instability escapes, making risk mapping easier.

**Stability Signature:** Variable  
**Binding Rule:** May bind only to spectral manifolds.
