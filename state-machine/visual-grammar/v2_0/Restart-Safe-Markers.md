## I. Human‑Readable Description  
Restart‑safe markers show **where the system can safely pause, resume, or checkpoint** without causing:

- altitude mixing  
- spectral leakage  
- traversal drift

- instability propagation  

They are the “softest” glyphs in the entire grammar layer — deliberately gentle, non‑hierarchical, and cognitively neutral.
