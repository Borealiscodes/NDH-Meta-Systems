## Structural Manifold Shapes v1.1  
These shapes are the **visual grammar primitives** for NDH manifolds.  
Each shape corresponds to a specific manifold class in your system.





---

### 1. **Structural Manifold** — **Hexagon**
- **Shape:** Regular hexagon  
- **Meaning:** Multi‑node structural systems with stable adjacency  
- **Why hexagon:**  
  - Six faces = six stable adjacency vectors  
  - No sharp corners = no trauma‑edge activation  
  - Symmetric = no hierarchy implied  
- **Used for:**  
  - TISD v1.1  
  - Corporate sub‑manifolds  
  - Traversal manifolds  

---

### 2. **Cognitive Manifold** — **Circle**
- **Shape:** Circle  
- **Meaning:** Altitude, perspective, mental framing  
- **Why circle:**  
  - No edges = no instability  
  - Perfect symmetry = altitude neutrality  
- **Used for:**  
  - Altitude icons  
  - Restart‑safe markers  

---

### 3. **Traversal Manifold** — **Arrow‑Loop**
- **Shape:** Loop with directional arrow  
- **Meaning:** Movement through spirals, transitions, flow  
- **Why arrow‑loop:**  
  - Represents non‑linear traversal  
  - Shows direction without implying hierarchy  
- **Used for:**  
  - Spiral flow glyphs  
  - PNG traversal diagram  





---

### 4. **Spectral Manifold** — **Triangle**
- **Shape:** Equilateral triangle  
- **Meaning:** Spectral operators (Δ, λ, R, T)  
- **Why triangle:**  
  - Three vertices = three spectral invariants  
  - Sharp edges = spectral sensitivity  
- **Used for:**  
  - Compression‑gate symbols  
  - Spectral‑geometry appendix  

---

### 5. **Risk Manifold** — **Broken Square**
- **Shape:** Square with one open side  
- **Meaning:** Instability, risk propagation, coercive vectors  
- **Why broken square:**  
  - Represents incomplete containment  
  - Shows where risk leaks into adjacent manifolds  
- **Used for:**  
  - Instability signatures  
  - Risk‑geometry companion study
