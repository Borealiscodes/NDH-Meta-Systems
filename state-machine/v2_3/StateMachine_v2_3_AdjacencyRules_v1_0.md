## ⭐ **2 — Adjacency Principles (v2.3)**

### **AP‑1 — Bounded Adjacency**  
No adjacency edge may expand, absorb, or create manifold drift.

### **AP‑2 — Reversible Adjacency**  
Every adjacency edge must support a reversible transition path.

### **AP‑3 — Ecosystem‑Safe Adjacency**  
Adjacency must respect NDH manifold boundaries and membrane purity.

### **AP‑4 — Expressive Neutrality**  
Adjacency edges cannot introduce expressive drift or hierarchy.

### **AP‑5 — Temporal Neutrality**  
Adjacency edges cannot introduce temporal load or epoch formation.

### **AP‑6 — Herd‑Ecology Neutrality**  
Adjacency edges cannot create herd-governance authority gradients.
