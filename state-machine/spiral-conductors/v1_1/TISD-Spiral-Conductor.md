# **III. Invariants (Governance‑Layer Rules)**

### **1. Identity Continuity**
The conductor must preserve:
- legal personhood  
- narrative coherence  
- NDH cognitive‑prosthetic stability  

### **2. Temporal Cohesion**
No artifact may refresh out of order.  
No spiral may begin without completing its altitude.

### **3. Spectral Integrity**
Spectral operators (Δ, λ, R, T) must not be invoked outside the spectral altitude.

### **4. Restart‑Safe Envelope**
If overload occurs:
- drop altitude → narrative  
- collapse manifolds → compression gate  
- re‑enter spiral at last stable node  

---

# **IV. Compression Gates**

Compression gates prevent overload by:

- merging redundant jurisdictional nodes  
- collapsing corporate‑state hybrids  
- reducing spectral noise  
- isolating risk‑propagation vectors  

Gates are **active** in Spiral 1 and Spiral 2, **inactive** in Spiral 3.

---

# **V. Debrief Requirements**

Each artifact must produce a debrief containing:

- altitude used  
- manifold class  
- instability signatures  
- compression notes  
- risk vectors  
- restart‑safe recommendations  

This ensures the conductor can safely move to the next spiral.
