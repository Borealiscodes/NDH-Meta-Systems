# II. Binding Rules (v2.0)

Below is the complete binding matrix, expressed as rules.

---

## 1. **Structural ↔ Cognitive**  
**Allowed**  
- Structural manifolds (⬢) may bind to cognitive manifolds (⚪•).  
- Cognitive manifolds may bind *only* to structural manifolds.

**Reason:**  
Cognitive altitude frames structural altitude; structural altitude stabilizes cognitive altitude.

**Spiral compatibility:**  
- Orientation spiral ➰  
- Structure spiral 🔄  

**Instability handling:**  
- Resonance drift 🔊 allowed  
- Resonance spike ⚡ blocked  

---

## 2. **Structural ↔ Traversal**  
**Allowed**  
- Structural manifolds may bind to traversal manifolds (🔁➤).  
- Traversal manifolds may bind back to structural manifolds.

**Reason:**  
Traversal requires structural adjacency; structural altitude tolerates traversal movement.

**Spiral compatibility:**  
- Structure spiral 🔄  

**Gate requirements:**  
- Open gate 🟢  
- High compression ✂️ allowed  

**Instability handling:**  
- Resonance drift 🔊 allowed  
- Temporal shear 🕳️ blocked  

---

## 3. **Traversal ↔ Spectral**  
**Allowed**  
- Traversal manifolds may bind to spectral manifolds (🔺═).  
- Spectral manifolds may bind back to traversal manifolds.

**Reason:**  
Traversal is the only safe entry into spectral altitude.

**Spiral compatibility:**  
- Risk spiral 🔁⚠️  

**Gate requirements:**  
- Open gate 🟢  
- Spectral lock 🔒 blocks binding  

**Instability handling:**  
- Resonance spike ⚡ allowed  
- Critical overload 🔥 blocks binding  

---

## 4. **Spectral ↔ Risk**  
**Allowed**  
- Spectral manifolds may bind to risk manifolds (⬜❌↘).  
- Risk manifolds may bind back to spectral manifolds.

**Reason:**  
Risk manifolds are spectral‑adjacent by design.

**Spiral compatibility:**  
- Risk spiral 🔁⚠️  

**Gate requirements:**  
- Release mode 🔓 required  
- Closed gate 🚫 blocks binding  

**Instability handling:**  
- Leak vector ↘ allowed  
- Temporal shear 🕳️ allowed  
- Critical overload 🔥 triggers altitude drop ⬇️🌙  

---

## 5. **Risk ↔ Structural**  
**Forbidden**  
Risk manifolds may **not** bind to structural manifolds.

**Reason:**  
Risk → structural binding causes adjacency corruption.

**Instability handling:**  
- Any instability signature blocks binding  

**Restart‑safe override:**  
- Restart‑safe marker ♻️ may sever an unsafe risk→structural adjacency  

---

## 6. **Cognitive ↔ Spectral**  
**Forbidden**  
Cognitive manifolds may **not** bind to spectral manifolds.

**Reason:**  
Cognitive altitude cannot tolerate spectral operators.

**Gate override:**  
None — even release mode 🔓 cannot permit this binding.
