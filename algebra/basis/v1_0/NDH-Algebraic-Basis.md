# II. Element Sets (Basis Classes)

NDH has five fundamental element sets:

### 1. **Manifold Class**  
The typed “spaces” NDH operates in.

\[
\mathcal{M} = \{\text{cognitive}, \text{structural}, \text{traversal}, \text{spectral}, \text{risk}\}
\]

Each manifold is a **typed element**.

---

### 2. **Gate Class**  
The algebra’s operator‑state set.

\[
\mathcal{G} = \{CG0, CG1, CG\Delta, CGR, CG\lambda\}
\]

Gates are **operators**, not manifolds.

---

### 3. **Instability Class**  
The algebra’s constraint set.

\[
\mathcal{I} = \{\text{drift}, \text{leak}, \text{shear}, \text{overload}\}
\]

Instability signatures behave like **error operators**.

---

### 4. **Restart‑Safe Class**  
The algebra’s identity elements.

\[
\mathcal{R} = \{\text{restart\_safe}, \text{state\_box}, \text{resume\_point}\}
\]

These are **idempotent**.

---

### 5. **Zero Class**  
The algebra’s zero elements.

\[
\mathcal{Z} = \{\text{drop\_altitude}, \text{spectral\_lock}\}
\]

Zero elements annihilate morphisms.

# IV. Closure Rules (v1.0)

### Closure Rule 1 — Manifold Closure  
Manifolds are closed under altitude transitions:

\[
\forall m \in \mathcal{M},\ \exists a \in \mathcal{A} : m \xrightarrow{a} m'
\]

---

### Closure Rule 2 — Gate Closure  
Gates are closed under operator composition:

\[
CG1 \circ CG\Delta = CG\Delta
\]

\[
CG0 \circ CGR = CG0
\]

---

### Closure Rule 3 — Instability Closure  
Instability signatures are closed under restart‑safe identity:

\[
i \circ \text{restart\_safe} = \text{restart\_safe}
\]

---

### Closure Rule 4 — Zero Closure  
Zero elements annihilate morphisms:

\[
\text{drop\_altitude} \circ f = \text{drop\_altitude}
\]

\[
\text{spectral\_lock} \circ f = \text{spectral\_lock}
\]

# V. Identity Elements (Idempotent)

Identity elements satisfy:

\[
r \circ r = r
\]

Where:

- restart_safe  
- state_box  
- resume_point  

are all idempotent.

These are the algebra’s **restart‑safe anchors**.

# VI. Zero Elements (Absorbing)

Zero elements satisfy:

\[
z \circ x = z
\]

Where:

- drop_altitude  
- spectral_lock  

are absorbing.

These are the algebra’s **halt operators**.
