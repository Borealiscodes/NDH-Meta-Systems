### 🌱 Meta‑Systems Seed v1.0

**Core Intent:**  
Design systems that *govern how systems are designed*—without becoming coercive, hierarchical, or opaque.

**Primitive Axes:**
- **Axis M1 — Clarity:**  
  Systems must be legible, inspectable, and non‑recursive in their core logic.
- **Axis M2 — Non‑Authority:**  
  No system may claim inherent right to direct, only to *coordinate*.
- **Axis M3 — Membrane:**  
  Boundaries between systems are explicit, respected, and non‑leaky.
- **Axis M4 — Altitude:**  
  Distinguish between runtime, design, governance, and narrative layers.

**Seed Rule:**  
> Any new system must declare:  
> 1) its altitude,  
> 2) its membrane,  
> 3) its coupling,  
> 4) its non‑authority guarantees.

**Minimal Rehydrate Note:**  
“Meta‑Systems seed = a small set of rules that ensures every future system is:  
clarity‑aligned, non‑authority, membrane‑aware, and altitude‑declared.”

You can drop this into any future artifact and grow a full meta‑systems architecture from it.
