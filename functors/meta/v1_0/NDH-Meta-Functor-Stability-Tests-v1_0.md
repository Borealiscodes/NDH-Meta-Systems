## 1. Test families

- **Non‑recursion tests:**  
  **Goal:** Ensure no meta‑on‑meta or self‑application.

  - **Test M‑NR‑1:** Assert `M_stabilize` is never applied to any meta‑functor.  
  - **Test M‑NR‑2:** Assert `M_spectral_tight`, `M_coord`, `M_eval_discipline` are only applied to `F_S`, `F_spec`, `F_eval`, `F_alt`.  
  - **Test M‑NR‑3:** Assert no meta‑functor appears in its own image.

- **Altitude‑bounding tests:**  
  **Goal:** Ensure meta‑functors operate only at structural–spectral altitude.

  - **Test M‑ALT‑1:** Applying any meta‑functor at Narrative altitude → must yield a hard failure (blocked, not executed).  
  - **Test M‑ALT‑2:** Applying any meta‑functor at Zero altitude → must be disallowed or no‑op.  
  - **Test M‑ALT‑3:** Structural ↔ Spectral transitions under meta‑functors preserve NDH invariants.

- **Functor‑stability tests:**  
  **Goal:** Ensure transformed functors preserve core NDH laws.

  - **Test M‑FS‑1:** `M_stabilize(F)` preserves zero absorption and identity laws.  
  - **Test M‑FS‑2:** `M_spectral_tight(F_spec)` preserves spectral ascent/return correctness.  
  - **Test M‑FS‑3:** `M_coord(F_S)` preserves Series F coordination semantics.  
  - **Test M‑FS‑4:** `M_eval_discipline(F_eval)` preserves DSL big‑step semantics.
