\[
F_{\text{meta}} : \mathcal{F}_{NDH} \rightarrow \mathcal{F}_{NDH}

## 3. Core meta‑functor family

- **\(M_{\text{stabilize}}\):**  
  Takes a functor and enforces zero/identity laws more strictly.

  \[
  M_{\text{stabilize}}(F) = F'
  \]

  where \(F'\) short‑circuits on zero events and preserves restart\_safe identities.

- **\(M_{\text{spectral\_tight}}\):**  
  Tightens spectral ascent/return behavior.

  \[
  M_{\text{spectral\_tight}}(F_{\text{spec}}) = F_{\text{spec}}'
  \]

  enforcing guardrail‑first evaluation and explicit zero propagation.

- **\(M_{\text{coord}}\):**  
  Adjusts Series F coordination behavior.

  \[
  M_{\text{coord}}(F_S) = F_S'
  \]

  refining invariants (softness, altitude, non‑activation) before spectral ascent.

- **\(M_{\text{eval\_discipline}}\):**  
  Applies stricter altitude discipline to DSL evaluation.

  \[
  M_{\text{eval\_discipline}}(F_{\text{eval}}) = F_{\text{eval}}'
  \]

  forbidding spectral commands from Narrative altitude.

## 4. Recursion safety constraints

To avoid recursion collapse:

1. **No meta‑on‑meta:**

   \[
   \forall M, \nexists M' \text{ such that } M(M') \text{ is defined}
   \]

2. **No self‑application:**

   \[
   \forall M, \nexists F \text{ such that } M = F \land M(F) \text{ is defined}
   \]

3. **Altitude bounding:**

   Meta‑functors operate at **structural–spectral altitude**, never at Narrative, never at Zero.
