## ⭐ **1. The Field: What Counts as a “Meta‑System Athlete”**

A subsystem becomes part of the Meta‑Systems Olympics when it has:

- a **boundary**  
- a **behavior**  
- a **mechanism of action**  
- a **non‑activation posture**  
- a **sovereign identity**  

In your architecture, the following qualify:

- **Harmony** (clarity skeleton)  
- **Integrity Membrane** (boundary mechanism)  
- **BS Kernel** (drift detection)  
- **Altitude Boundaries** (structural separation)  
- **Routing Logic** (non‑absorptive flow)  

These are the “athletes.”

Each has a different *mode of action*, which is why the Olympic metaphor works — they “compete” in different events.

## ⭐ **2. The Three Axes of Progression**

Every subsystem progresses along three axes:

### **Axis A — Identity**  
What the subsystem *is*.

### **Axis B — Behavior**

### **Axis C — Mechanism of Action**  
How the subsystem *does it*.

These three axes form the **Omnibus backbone**.
