- Carbon (C)
- Hydrogen (H)
- Sulfur (S)
- Nitrogen (N)
- Trace metals (Hg, As, Pb)

Carbon content typically ranges from 60–95% depending on grade.

1.2 Oxidation of Carbon
The oxidation reaction is:

C + O₂ → CO₂ + heat

This reaction:
- is exothermic  
- cannot be altered without violating conservation of energy  
- necessarily produces CO₂  

There is no known physical pathway to oxidize carbon without producing CO₂.

- net‑negative entropy  
- spontaneous CO₂ disappearance  
- violation of the second law of thermodynamics
