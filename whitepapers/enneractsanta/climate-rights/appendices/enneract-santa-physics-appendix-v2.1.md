⚛️ 1. Thermodynamic Constraints

`md

1.1 Coal Composition
Coal contains:
- 60–95% carbon
- hydrogen
- sulfur
- nitrogen
- trace metals (Hg, As, Pb)

1.2 Carbon Oxidation
C + O₂ → CO₂ + heat

This reaction:
- is unavoidable
- is exothermic
- cannot be altered without violating conservation of energy

1.3 Entropy Constraints
Any process claiming “zero‑emission coal” requires:
- net‑negative entropy
- spontaneous CO₂ disappearance
- violation of the second law of thermodynamics

Thus, “clean coal” is thermodynamically impossible.

🧪 3. Combustion Chemistry Constraints

`md

3.1 Sulfur and Nitrogen
Coal combustion produces:
- SO₂ → acid rain precursor
- NOₓ → ozone and smog precursor

Removing these requires:
- pre‑combustion chemical processing
- more energy than coal yields

3.2 Trace Metals
Coal releases:
- mercury
- arsenic
- lead

Capturing these requires:
- high‑temperature plasma scrubbing
- energy‑intensive filtration
- still does not eliminate CO₂

Thus, coal cannot be chemically “clean.”

⚙️ 4. Energy‑System Constraints

`md

4.1 Net Energy Penalty
“Clean coal” systems require:
- carbon capture
- sulfur scrubbing
- nitrogen removal
- particulate filtration
- trace metal capture

These impose a 20–40% parasitic energy load (CCS/CCUS).

Coal becomes net‑negative.

4.2 Carbon Capture Limits
CCS systems:
- capture < 90% CO₂
- require massive energy input
- degrade sorbents over time
- do not eliminate other pollutants

4.3 Failure of Actual “Clean Coal” Infrastructure

CCS (Carbon Capture and Storage)
Fails because:
- amine scrubbing requires high regeneration heat
- sorbent degradation reduces capture efficiency
- compression + transport + injection consumes huge energy

CCUS (Utilization)
Fails because:
- CO₂ “utilization” markets are tiny
- most utilization re‑releases CO₂

IGCC (Integrated Gasification Combined Cycle)
Fails because:
- gasification is energy‑intensive
- syngas cleanup is incomplete
- still emits CO₂

HELE (High‑Efficiency Low‑Emissions)
Fails because:
- “low emissions” still means high CO₂
- efficiency gains cannot overcome carbon oxidation physics

Scrubbers
Fails because:
- remove SO₂ but not CO₂
- produce toxic sludge waste streams

Electrostatic Precipitators / Baghouse Filters
Fails because:
- remove particulates only
- do nothing for CO₂, SO₂, NOₓ, Hg, As, Pb

Sequestration Wells
Fails because:
- leakage risk persists
- pressure buildup destabilizes formations
- long‑term monitoring is energy‑prohibitive

Thus, the infrastructure itself is physically impossible as a “cleaning” mechanism.
`
