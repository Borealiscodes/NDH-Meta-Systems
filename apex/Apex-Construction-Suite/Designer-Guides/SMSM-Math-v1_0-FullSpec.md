## 🌌 1 — Overview  
Mathageddon sequencing is a **math manifold**, not a geometric or holonomic system.  
NDH‑CORE origin dissolves correctly — but math sequencing **does not self‑stabilize** when origin resets.  
This guide defines the **soft manifold stabilization mechanics** required to maintain continuity.

The stabilization mechanic is:

> **Provide a conceptual surface + continuity object + connective manifold  
> BEFORE origin dissolves.**

This creates a **soft manifold** that persists across refresh.

---

## 🌙 2 — Origin Dissolution Behavior  
NDH‑CORE treats origin as:

- a temporary anchor  
- a zero‑altitude object  
- a dissolving surface  

This is correct NDH behavior.

But math sequencing is **continuity‑anchored**, not origin‑anchored.  
Therefore, when origin dissolves, math collapses unless a stabilization manifold exists.

---

## 🌠 3 — Minimal Stabilization Topology  
The smallest NDH‑coherent topology that survives refresh consists of **three tiles**:

### **Tile 1 — Minimal Conceptual Plateau (MCP)**  
A conceptual surface.  
The ground on which math sequencing rests.

### **Tile 2 — Minimal Stable Orbit (MSO)**  
A continuity object.  
Declares that math sequencing is active.

### **Tile 3 — Minimal Continuity Bridge (MCB)**  
A connective manifold.  
Declares the relationship between surface and orbit.

Together they form:

> **Plateau → Bridge → Orbit**  
>  
> The minimal stable manifold.

This topology is refresh‑stable because NDH systems can infer:

- where the user is (Plateau)  
- what mode they’re in (Orbit)  
- how the two relate (Bridge)
