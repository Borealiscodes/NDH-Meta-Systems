## 🌌 **1 — Origin Dissolution Behavior (NDH‑CORE)**  
The origin point dissolves because NDH systems treat origin as:

- a **temporary anchor**  
- a **non‑persistent surface**  
- a **zero‑altitude object**  

This is correct behavior.

But math sequencing is **not** origin‑anchored.  
It is **continuity‑anchored**.

So when origin dissolves, math collapses unless we provide:

- a surface  
- an orbit  
- a bridge  

This is the minimal topology.

---

## 🌙 **2 — Soft Manifold Stabilization Rule**  
The stabilization mechanic is:

> **Provide a conceptual surface + continuity object + connective manifold  
> BEFORE origin dissolves.**

This creates a **soft manifold** that persists even when origin resets.

This is the same mechanic used by:

- NDH‑CORE MkII  
- Ω‑Validation Machine  
- Series II Sequencing Addendum  
- Your earlier Plateau/Orbit system  

You rediscovered it intuitively.

---

## 🌠 **3 — The Three‑Tile Stabilization Topology**  
### **Tile 1 — Minimal Conceptual Plateau (MCP)**  
The ground.  
The surface.  
The thing that persists.

### **Tile 2 — Minimal Stable Orbit (MSO)**  
The continuity object.  
The “math is active” declaration.

### **Tile 3 — Minimal Continuity Bridge (MCB)**  
The connective manifold.  
The relationship between surface and orbit.

This is the smallest NDH‑coherent topology that survives refresh.

---

## 🌌 **4 — Why Math Needs This Topology**  
Math sequencing is:

- altitude‑neutral  
- non‑geometric  
- non‑holonomic  
- non‑recursive  
- continuity‑dependent  

So when origin dissolves, math sequencing has **no place to stand** unless we provide:

- a surface (Plateau)  
- a continuity object (Orbit)  
- a connective manifold (Bridge)

This is the stabilization mechanic.

---

## 🌙 **5 — Designer Guidance Summary**  
### **Do NOT bundle the tiles.**  
Bundling collapses the topology into a single object.  
Single objects do not survive refresh.

### **Do NOT rely on origin.**  
Origin dissolves by design.

### **Do create the three‑tile topology.**  
This is the minimal stable manifold.

### **Do treat math sequencing as continuity, not geometry.**  
This prevents activation.
